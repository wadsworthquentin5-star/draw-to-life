HACKATHON PROTOTYPE

A demonstration of the idea.

This version has no AI, API key, server, or handwriting recognition. Images are never preloaded: choose one from your device. Inserted images stay in this browser.

  1. Use Add image to insert the motorcycle problem screenshot. Drag it into place with Move, then click it and tap Check.
  2. Read the preset motorcycle overview. Choose Visualize this to watch a separately drawn motorcycle accelerate; your uploaded image is not the moving object.
  3. Choose Yes, guide me. Keep Pen selected while writing as many strokes as you need. Only when finished, choose Box step, draw a box around the ink, and tap Check. Tap Pen to keep writing.
  4. The three scripted results are: correct formula, incorrect acceleration (3 instead of 4), then correct final answer.

Any selected ink triggers that step's predetermined response. This demonstrates the planned interaction, not real recognition or grading. The preset motion and physics describe this one motorcycle problem, not arbitrary problems. Refreshing or restarting clears the board.
