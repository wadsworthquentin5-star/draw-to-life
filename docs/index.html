HACKATHON PROTOTYPE

Space to think. Room to explore.

The whiteboard has no fixed page edges. Use Move and drag blank space to pan. Pinch with two fingers or use − / ＋ to zoom. Tap the percentage for 100% or Fit all to find all your work.

Add as many reference images as you need, one at a time. With Move selected, drag an image to place it; select it and drag its corner handle to resize. Adding an image does not erase your work.

For Apple Pencil, enable Pencil only: Pencil writes and a finger moves the canvas. Two fingers pan and zoom. Keep Pen selected while writing; choose Box step and draw a freehand circle around your work only when ready to check.

  1. Use Add image to insert the motorcycle problem screenshot. Select its rim with Move, then tap the black Check bubble.
  2. Read the preset overview and choose Visualize this. A separately drawn motorcycle accelerates; your reference image stays on the board.
  3. Choose Yes, guide me. Write the velocity formula, choose Box step, circle it with a freehand loop, and tap Check. Tap Pen to continue writing. Step 3 uses the distance formula.
  4. The three physics responses confirm the formula, correct an acceleration of 3 to 4 m/s², then confirm 38 m travelled. The final position is 43 m east of the sign.
  5. Choose Next: chemistry and manually insert the provided atom problem image. Check it and visualize how the first three shells contribute capacities of 2, 8, and 18.
  6. For chemistry guidance, first write n = 3 for the first shell to demonstrate the planned mistake. Check explains why the first shell needs n = 1. Retry with n = 1, then work through n = 2, n = 3, and the total 2 + 8 + 18 = 28.

No AI, API key, server, or handwriting recognition is used. Any selected ink triggers that stage's predetermined response, including the planned chemistry retry.
