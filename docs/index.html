HACKATHON PROTOTYPE

Space to think. Room to explore.

The whiteboard has no fixed page edges. Use Move and drag blank space to pan. Pinch with two fingers or use − / ＋ to zoom. Tap the percentage for 100% or Fit all to find all your work.

Add as many reference images as you need, one at a time. With Move selected, drag an image to place it; select it and drag its corner handle to resize. Adding an image does not erase your work.

For Apple Pencil, enable Pencil only: Pencil writes and a finger moves the canvas. Two fingers pan and zoom. Keep Pen selected while writing; use Box step only when ready to check.

  1. Use Add image to insert the motorcycle problem screenshot. Select its rim with Move, then tap the black Check bubble.
  2. Read the preset overview and choose Visualize this. A separately drawn motorcycle accelerates; your reference image stays on the board.
  3. Choose Yes, guide me. Write your formula, select Box step, enclose it, and tap Check. Tap Pen to continue writing.
  4. The three scripted results are: correct formula, incorrect acceleration (3 instead of 4), then correct final answer.

No AI, API key, or handwriting recognition is used. Any selected ink triggers that step's predetermined response. The lesson describes this one motorcycle problem, not arbitrary images. Images are never preloaded or sent to an AI service. Refreshing or restarting clears the board.
